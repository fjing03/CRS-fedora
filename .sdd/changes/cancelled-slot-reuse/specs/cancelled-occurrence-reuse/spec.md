# Delta specification — cancelled occurrence reuse

Date: 2026-11-13
Batch 3: frozen after independent review Round 1; declarative carry-forwards appended below. Proposal and design are frozen. No implementation approval.

SHALL means required behaviour, not permission to execute it. Scenario ids below are the implementation/test trace keys. No app/test execution, demo write, commit or push is approved by this spec.

## R1. Original reservation ownership (design D3)

**S01 — original cells only.** Given an owned recurring class at a valid week/day with four occupied 30-minute original cells and no request ownership/lifecycle contradiction, when that occurrence is cancelled, those four cells SHALL become available, lose their original `class_session_id`, and each gain exactly one version. Its recurring session and every other week SHALL stay unchanged. A 30-minute and a one-hour class SHALL obey the same rule.

**S02 — already available and duplicate available.** Given original coordinates whose rows are all available, including schema-legal duplicate available rows, cancellation SHALL record its normal exception/history but SHALL not rewrite those slots, clear stale ids, or bump versions. Duplicate available rows SHALL be reported as no-ops, not repaired. An original/available mixture SHALL release only the original subset.

**S03 — newer request wins.** Given a matching old class id on a cell now held by a pending/approved request through a junction or the documented junction-less anchor/span fallback, original release SHALL preserve that request, links, slot status and version. This SHALL hold for another class and the exact same source class. An original/available/proven request-held mixture SHALL release only the original subset.

**S04 — ambiguity/missing refuses, not guesses.** Missing expected rows, duplicate coordinates involving a non-available row, mismatched occupied owner, unexplained pending state, orphan/inactive links, malformed or contradictory claims, and request-lifecycle evidence inconsistent with recurring ownership SHALL cause runtime cancellation to refuse the whole selected batch before any lasting write. Cleanup SHALL report/exclude the entire ambiguous occurrence. Out-of-range rows carrying the source id SHALL be reported and not freed. A test SHALL show that a matching stale class id does not override contradictory audit evidence.

## R2. Atomic cancellation and request handling (D4)

**S05 — single/day scope.** Single and selected-day cancellation SHALL use the same occurrence-release rule. The opened class plus checked class rows SHALL be cancelled; unchecked classes SHALL remain. The reason SHALL not create a whole-date lecturer ban. Invalid ownership, reason or notice SHALL retain the existing refusal behaviour. Repeating an already cancelled occurrence SHALL not create duplicate audits, exceptions, version bumps or notifications.

**S06 — attached requests.** Cancelling a source occurrence with an attached pending or approved request SHALL close the request and release only its proven landing cells, preserving existing audit/notification meaning. The approved path SHALL support occupied cells through the explicit cancellation-only policy. If newer/ambiguous ownership prevents safe attached-request release, the operation SHALL not free the newer booking; ambiguous ownership SHALL refuse atomically. Deleting junctions SHALL not make a landing cell look like an original reservation. Other ReleaseRequestSlots callers' strict/non-strict defaults SHALL remain unchanged.

**S07 — request-only day selections.** A checked make-up row SHALL withdraw only its pending request, never cancel its source class. A refused class batch SHALL not proceed to withdraw checked requests. A failed withdrawal SHALL roll back other withdrawals, class exceptions, original/landing releases and audits from that selection. In the success case all selected changes SHALL commit together.

**S08 — rollback/notification boundary.** A conditional flip failure or audit exception at any point SHALL leave no partial slot, request or exception changes. Notifications SHALL be emitted only after the outermost successful commit, once per normal recipient/event; rollback SHALL send none. The cancellation remains final; no undo UI or endpoint SHALL be introduced. Its `original_release` audit receipt SHALL record each changed cell's identity/status/owner/version before and after, plus available/protected no-ops and their reasons, as D4 requires.

## R3. Reuse and approval agreement (D4)

**S09 — eligible reuse matrix.** After cancellation, an otherwise eligible source occurrence SHALL be able to request the released interval for: same room/different cohort; different room/different cohort; different room/same cohort; same lecturer/different course or cohort; same cancelled class/exact original room/week/day/time. Client green availability, server submission and approval validity SHALL agree. Every case remains subject to current independent checks and PL approval.

**S10 — independent blockers remain.** An active room booking, lecturer clash, any cohort clash, insufficient capacity, disallowed venue/module/type, holiday/Sunday restriction, notice failure, invalid span or existing active source request SHALL still refuse as today. The uncancelled occurrence at the same coordinates in another week SHALL still block. Freed cells SHALL have ordinary available styling with no cancellation note; a remaining independent blocker SHALL win.

**S11 — own landing cannot be re-released.** Given a cancelled class re-requested at its exact original range, approval SHALL retain occupied landing cells and all junctions, increment only the normal decision versions, and preserve the original cancellation reason/audit. Original release SHALL not free any landing cell, including cells with stale source ids. Partial-original overlaps SHALL protect the whole landing membership and release only independently proven remaining original cells. Source and target week SHALL not be confused. On an ordinary approval with no original exception, approval SHALL stamp the original exception once; when one exists it SHALL not replace its reason, including user-selected `official_event`.

**S12 — concurrency and lock lifetime.** Two eligible different-source submissions competing for the same released full span SHALL yield exactly one winner; the loser SHALL leave no partial hold or broken request links and SHALL retain its normal OCC conflict audit. Concurrent submit/cancel and approve/cancel on the same source SHALL serialize or refuse refresh/retry, not leave a request on an incorrectly released range. Source serialisation SHALL be keyed by the SOURCE class-session id, not anchor slot id. Advisory locks SHALL release after success, ordinary refusal and thrown exception. Submit/OCC SHALL retain the documented no-outer-transaction audit/compensation behaviour. Tests SHALL include a two-connection race, not only sequential mocks.

## R4. Independent, scoped event payloads (D5)

**S13 — cancellation provenance.** An original history event SHALL require the current exception plus a non-maintenance `class_cancelled` audit for the exact source occurrence. An approval-only `official_event` stamp SHALL not create cancellation history even when another class later overlaps the original range. User-selected `official_event` with cancellation audit SHALL qualify. Missing/contradictory provenance SHALL be reported, not invented. Cleanup and display SHALL use the same discriminator; maintenance receipts SHALL not create new source evidence.

**S14 — identity and full spans.** Payloads SHALL carry separate stable original-occurrence and request identities as specified in D5. Same coordinates, same source or same start SHALL not merge distinct events. Combined-cohort duplicates of one identity SHALL merge once. Original history SHALL survive cleared original slot ownership; active requests SHALL be read even with stale non-null class ids. Every landing SHALL retain its true full junction/mapped fallback span, not just its anchor cell. Existing modal id fields SHALL remain compatible. An original slot row claimed by an active request SHALL not also be emitted as a normal recurring event.

**S15 — scope/privacy.** Staff personal timetable SHALL include only owned records; venue only the chosen room; cohort only the chosen cohort; student only the student's cohort. Every relevant cancelled and new in-scope event SHALL remain available. Student payloads SHALL retain the current public ReasonLabel but omit staff-only free text/audit/request details. Other-staff public timetable blocks SHALL not grant remarks/private reasons/full-request access. Scope SHALL be applied before browser layout, not by hiding unrelated payload fields.

**S16 — status lifecycle and approved suppression.** Pending at an overlapping position SHALL coexist with original cancellation. Approval at an overlapping position SHALL keep cancellation above the approved block. If its own approved landing is non-overlapping but another in-scope active event overlaps that original, cancellation SHALL remain visible there. For a handled original with no in-scope active overlap, today's suppression SHALL remain unchanged. Approval-stamped originals without user-cancellation evidence SHALL always retain their existing suppression. Withdrawal, rejection or automatic rejection SHALL remove only the active landing block, with no lost cancellation history or stuck reservation.

## R5. Shared stack layout and interaction (D6)

**S17 — exact/partial/multiple/adjacent.** For 30-minute, one-hour and two-hour events, exact and partial overlaps SHALL produce independently readable vertical lanes, cancelled above every directly overlapping active block. Multiple cancellations and multiple active events SHALL keep all identities, true start/end positions and durations. Adjacent half-open intervals SHALL be allowed to share a lane. Reordering identical input SHALL not change lane assignments; same-group ties SHALL follow D6's start/end/eventKey comparator. Non-overlapping components SHALL reuse lanes. A normal no-overlap day SHALL keep its existing height unless a venue selectable base row is needed.

**S18 — shared render contract.** Staff My Timetable, Venue Timetable, Cohort Timetable, Student My Timetable and permitted student cohort view SHALL consume the same lane behaviour, including venue's custom-cell path. No renderer SHALL overwrite a same-start event. A day header SHALL span its physical lane rows and the time grid SHALL remain aligned. Extra rows SHALL not introduce extra event identities.

**S19 — click/focus/jumps.** Each event SHALL have its own focusable target and correct details popup. Click, Enter and Space SHALL open that event; Escape SHALL close and return focus normally. A request jump SHALL select its request block; an original-occurrence jump SHALL select that occurrence/week, not the same-cell neighbour. Independent original/landing schedules and full spans SHALL remain correct in modals. Existing details width (680px), footer sizing and status/ownership colours SHALL remain unchanged.

**S20 — real venue availability.** Cancelled history SHALL not prevent selecting a truly free room cell or limit a selectable span. Where it covers that cell visually, the venue view SHALL expose a separate base selectable row as D6 specifies. Selection SHALL use actual status/normal eligibility, ignore history lanes, and remain unavailable under independent blockers. Arrangement itself SHALL remain an ordinary booking grid, not a stacked history view.

**S21 — responsive/themes/print.** At desktop (1440px), tablet (768px) and phone (390px), light and dark themes SHALL retain all relevant blocks, readable times/statuses and independent targets without clipping/painting over each other. Existing horizontal scrolling SHALL remain usable. Existing print output SHALL retain all lane rows and both records without fixed-height clipping; this SHALL not introduce timetable-print-v2 features.

## R6. Honest summaries (D7)

**S22 — classes and hours.** Cards SHALL derive from unique scoped event identities and true durations, not day/start coordinates, DOM blocks or lane count. Cancellation SHALL contribute zero taught classes/hours; pending SHALL count only in its existing pending share; confirmed = non-cancelled total minus pending; replacements = approved landing count. Conflict count SHALL include each event meeting the current conflict/cancelled/holiday predicate once. My Teaching SHALL restrict the same data to the viewer's lecturer. Combined-cohort duplicates SHALL not double count, while distinct coincident identities SHALL not overwrite one another.

**S23 — venue union and unchanged baseline.** Venue occupied/free hours SHALL use a union of real pending/occupied room cells with the current open-day/holiday denominator and existing pending split. A cancellation 09–11 plus pending 09–11 SHALL contribute two pending hours, not four occupied hours. Base availability/lane rows SHALL contribute no teaching/room hours. No-reuse baseline card values SHALL remain identical. Tests SHALL cross-check PHP venue totals and JS cards against the same fixture facts.

## R7. Part B room-only explanations (D8)

**S24 — true active holder.** A room-only blocked cell SHALL explain its actual active holder even when the viewer shares neither lecturer nor cohort. Desktop and arrangement mobile SHALL show the permitted room, course/type, actual time, lecturer/cohort lines and public NORMAL/PENDING/REPLACEMENT state using the existing text-library vocabulary. Junction ownership SHALL outrank stale original ids. One holder repeated through different conflict arms SHALL be shown once; genuinely different holders SHALL remain distinct. No private reason, remarks, submission metadata or other-staff full-request action SHALL leak.

**S25 — presentation only and transitional fallback.** Tooltip composition SHALL keep classifyState precedence, cell labels, colours and eligibility unchanged. Text SHALL use escaped `data-tip` with real newline separators, never HTML, and new time strings SHALL use the shared 12-hour `~` format. A released cancelled occurrence SHALL never be named as an active holder. A still-occupied pre-cleanup cancelled original with no active holder, or unknown/ambiguous holder, SHALL show neutral Venue Conflict information rather than fabricated NORMAL history. Available cells SHALL have no new cancellation tooltip/badge. Part B SHALL not perform any cleanup or restore the old blocked-room business rule.

## R8. One-off existing-data cleanup (D9)

**S26 — dry-run inventory.** Default CLI invocation SHALL write nothing, including no audit/notification rows. It SHALL show live target identity, eligible/excluded cancellation pairs, each original coordinate/id/version, classification, request/junction/anchor/span protection, exact proposed fields and no-ops, plus a fingerprinted plan. It SHALL distinguish user cancellation evidence from approval-only, maintenance and undo tokens. Scope SHALL never be inferred from screenshots or fixed ids.

**S27 — target and authority gates.** Live apply SHALL allow only `class_replacement_fresh`; testing rehearsal SHALL explicitly select `class_replacement_testing`. Viva/demo/other targets, missing apply/target confirmation/approved plan/verified snapshot evidence SHALL fail closed. Before any real write the operator SHALL present current exact changes and obtain separate approval, after testing rehearsal and verified `pg_dump -Fc` / successful `pg_restore --list`. The snapshot SHALL be recorded with checksum/path and printed DB target. Planning/apply-code approval SHALL not imply cleanup-write approval. No demo run is part of automated acceptance tests.

**S28 — fingerprinted identity-scoped apply.** Under transaction locks, any changed scoped ownership input or missing planned identity SHALL abort the whole plan before lasting writes, requiring fresh inventory and approval. Only approved unambiguous occurrence identities SHALL change; ambiguous occurrences SHALL be reported/excluded as whole occurrences. Original cells SHALL become available/null-owner/version+1; available/request-held cells SHALL remain byte-identical in protected fields. Request status/links, source exception/reason, recurring schedule, other weeks and unrelated data SHALL remain. The legal maintenance audit SHALL hold operation identity and per-cell receipt without user notification. No broad update, reseed, data migration or page-read repair SHALL occur. The cleanup facility SHALL have no scheduled invocation or controller/render caller.

**S29 — repeat and receipt.** After apply, exact changed identities and no-ops, protected fields/versions, links, other weeks and row counts SHALL be verified. With no new cancellations, a second inventory SHALL propose no further changes and a repeat apply SHALL not bump versions or duplicate maintenance work. The operation receipt SHALL be appended to docs/demo-db-data-changes.md without staging another session's rows.

**S30 — recovery after drift/reuse.** A separately approved pre-reuse recovery SHALL restore only receipt cells still available with exact post-owner/version and no live junction/anchor/span ownership, incrementing current versions and preserving cancellation/history. If ANY receipt cell has drifted/reused, the operation SHALL make no partial restore: stop, report affected bookings and request recovery instructions. Whole-DB snapshot restoration over newer work and implicit reactivation of cancelled classes SHALL be forbidden.

## R9. Verification and stop boundary (D10)

All write fixtures SHALL use the explicit testing DB and guarded 8030 Playwright target. Dates SHALL be frozen in PHP tests; browser pending fixtures SHALL avoid the live testing checker's expiry window and verify their own ids/status before assertions. The checker SHALL not be changed silently. RefreshDatabase and valid 30-minute/week/day fixtures SHALL be used. Independent release-helper default-policy regressions, advisory unlock paths and S25's pre-cleanup neutral-hover assertion SHALL be included.

Later implementation SHALL run lint, PHPStan with 1G, explicit-testing PHP tests, JS syntax, view compilation and scoped browser receipts, excluding TC70. Existing failures SHALL be disclosed separately. This specification review SHALL not run application tests or any cleanup, commit, push or Apply.
