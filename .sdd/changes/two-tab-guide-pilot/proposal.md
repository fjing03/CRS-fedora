# Two-tab guide pilot — Replacement Arrangement

## Scope and design
The user approved the wording for eight guides and authorized a Phase 3 pilot on Replacement Arrangement only. Replace its outdated inline help with the existing header guide trigger and shared dialog. No booking logic, DB changes, dependencies, or rollout to the other seven guides. Their legacy modal paths remain unchanged. The frozen fallback and separate `?` keyboard-shortcut overlay also stay unchanged. Copy baseline: the final Phase 1+2 Arrangement draft (chat message beginning “The phone check confirmed three useful details”).

Extend `ui-guide-modal` with optional content views keyed by two fixed tabs. The page-specific Blade content holds the approved copy without translating its paragraphs and topics into an array DSL. Reuse `modal-tabs` and the existing dialog/Close/Summary-lock behavior. Keep the header, tabs and Close outside the scrollable `.modal-body` pane. Scope the 48×48 coarse-pointer minimum to the pilot opener, tabs and close controls. Keep focus within the open pilot, restore it to the opener on close, and guard both Arrangement key handlers (including the capture-phase shared week guard) while the guide is open. Escape should close only the guide, even after its visible state is cleared by the shared listener. Scope typography/layout to opted-in guides; keep status comparisons readable on narrow screens and use theme tokens. GuideModal owns selection and scroll reset, not the booking page. No persisted tab state.

## Acceptance criteria / tasks
1. Wire only Replacement Arrangement and preserve the approved wording: steps and troubleshooting in Getting started; grouped rules, slot states and deadline in Full reference. Both explain automatic rejection and the notice rule.
2. Opening always shows Getting started from the top; switching shows only the selected content and resets `.modal-body.scrollTop`. Both tabs and Close remain usable while reading. Existing close paths and legacy guide behavior stay intact. Verify keyboard tab activation, Escape and week shortcuts do not change the booking week or slot selection.
3. Verify desktop/light+dark and phone/light+dark (including a narrow, short phone), actual tap, target sizes, reading scroll, reopening/reset and no horizontal overflow. Verify both flat and grouped legacy guides still open without tabs. Do not submit requests or mutate data. Record checks and update the Arrangement page changelog. Stop before rollout.
