# UI drift survey — 2026-11-17 (pass 1, and pass 2 in §6)

**What this is.** The live surfaces compared against their drawings, at four widths, in both
themes at the extremes, across roles. The method and vocabulary are fixed in `CONTEXT.md`;
the decisions that produced this shape were grilled and agreed on 2026-11-17.

**Read this first: pass 1 covers 5 of the 10 surfaces.** What is *not* yet compared is listed
in §5 with the reason, so nothing here reads as "the rest is clean". Coverage is a fact about
this document, not a claim about the app.

- Live captures: `ui-prototype/ui-drift-2026-11-17/live/` — 60 PNGs (10 surfaces × 390/768/1024/1440, both themes at 390 and 1440)
- Drawing captures: `ui-prototype/ui-drift-2026-11-17/drawing/` — 60 PNGs, same widths
- Crops for the findings below: `ui-prototype/ui-drift-2026-11-17/crops/`
- Pictures are on disk and out of git, per `ui-prototype/.gitignore`; this report is committed.

**Buckets.** *Broken* — someone can't read or reach it. *Inconsistent* — the same component
two ways. *Taste* — no reference says it's wrong. **Effort**: S = under an hour, M = an
afternoon, L = needs a small lane.

## 1. Findings

### Broken

**B1 — FIXED 2026-11-17 (same day).** A phone card on My Replacements had no `ORIGINAL` /
`REQUESTED` block and dropped the requested slot entirely. *Effort was L; it turned out to be
an S because the drawing already carried the implementation as a page-local wrapper
(`wrapHomeCard` + its `.cl-rc-*` block) — ported verbatim into `replacement-home.blade.php`,
"wrapped, not edited", exactly as the drawing intended. Verified at 390 in both themes under
a real touch context: 5 cards, 1 with a REQUESTED band, 4 with the "Not requested"
placeholder, none on the desktop table (`tests/replacement-home-status-badges.spec.ts`
12 passed / 1 skipped after re-pointing its badge selector from `.rc-header` to `.cl-rc-foot`).*
The original finding follows, unchanged.

The drawing lays each card out as two labelled rows — `ORIGINAL` (week, date, deadline,
venue) and, when a request exists, `REQUESTED` (the same four facts for the new slot) — with
the conflict-reason chip and the cohort on their own line. Live flattens everything into
prose and prints **no requested slot at all**: a reader on a phone cannot see *when* the
replacement is being asked for, which is the single most important fact on a pending card.

Evidence: `crops/a-live-phone-card.png` vs `crops/a-drawing-phone-card.png`; after the fix,
`crops/a-live-phone-card-fixed.png` and `…-fixed-light.png`.
Live markup: `.replacement-card` still carries `cell-day-date`, `cell-time`, `cell-dash`
(desktop table cells), so the phone layout is a restyle of the table rather than the
drawing's own card body.

**B2 — WITHDRAWN 2026-11-17, same day. It was a flaw in my method, not in the page.**

The finding was "Hover a colour to learn more prints on a touch phone". It does not:
`ui-legend-bar.blade.php` already carries both strings, and `theme.css:2253/2261-2262` swaps
them under `(hover: none)`. Measured with a real touch context
(`hasTouch`, `isMobile`, mobile UA, 390 wide): `hoverSpan: none, touchSpan: inline`,
`matchMedia('(hover: hover)').matches === false` → the page says **"Tap a colour to learn
more"**. With a mouse at 390 it says "Hover", which is correct.

**The method consequence, which matters for pass 2:** a 390-wide capture with a mouse is not
a phone. Every state that depends on pointer capability — hover copy, 48 × 48 touch targets,
`:hover` styling — must be captured with `hasTouch: true, isMobile: true` or it will report
the desktop behaviour at phone width. Pass 1 captured the phone band with a mouse, so **every
finding whose litmus is "on a phone" needs re-checking under touch**. B1 is unaffected: it is
a structural difference visible in either mode.

**B3 — FIXED 2026-11-17 (same day). Request Approval's phone card never showed the requested
slot, and printed the ORIGINAL class date under a bare `Date:` label.** *Effort was S.* The
drawing carried the implementation (`wrapRequestCard` in `ui-prototype/my-replacements.html`),
ported page-locally: the card now heads with `#id` pill · proposer · status, shows one labelled
**REQUESTED** band (week pill, date, countdown, time, venue + the slot-validity ✓/⚠), and a
foot with the course and the request age. Verified at 390: `#26 · Surayaini Binti Basri ·
Rejected`, `REQUESTED Week 7`, `Fri, 6 Nov 2026`, `2:00–3:00 PM`, `B010 ✓`,
`AMSE2003 (P) | 15 days ago`.
Caveat that matters: today the queue has **no pending rows**, so this card has only been seen
with decided rows; the pending shape is the drawing's, unexercised against live data.

**B4 — FIXED 2026-11-17 (same day). Class Replacements' phone card was a tower of labels, not
the drawing's comparison.** *Effort was S, and CSS-only plus a small DOM patch.* Ported from
`ui-prototype/student-class-replacements.html` (its phone band + `patchSlotRows`/`shortTime`):
`ORIGINAL` / `REQUESTED` now sit side by side under one tiny label each with the week in its
own pill, the four facts form a 2 × 2 (date over time, countdown over venue), the header's bare
week number is gone, and a time range with one meridiem prints it once (`2:00–4:00 PM`).
Verified at 390: 10 cards, 10 week pills, labels `ORIGINAL Week 7` / `REQUESTED`.

## 2. What the numbers said that the eye could not

The fingerprint pass compared 189 shared classes between live and drawing at 1440 and 390, on
computed font size, weight, padding, colour, border, radius, display, gap and height. Two
lessons worth keeping:

- **Most "differences" were structure, not styling.** `.cell-code` and `.rc-code` differ in
  `display` and width because the live phone card is a restyled table where the drawing has a
  purpose-built card body — the same root cause as B1. A class-by-class diff cannot tell you
  that; only the picture could.
- **The drawing's baseline is older than at least one live law** (the 48 × 48 touch rule).
  Any future pass should date-stamp the drawing's decisions against the rulebook, or it will
  keep reporting live improvements as drift.

## 6. Pass 2 (2026-11-17, evening) — five surfaces, reviewed through `impeccable`

**Method.** Two assessments kept apart until both were done, as the critique skill requires:

- **A — design review**, by a separate read-only process (no coding tools): judgement on
  specificity, the ten Nielsen heuristics, cognitive load, the emotional shape, and the issues.
- **B — mechanical detector**, `impeccable detect --json` over the four timetable blades and the
  arrangement blade. **Result: clean, zero findings.** Everything below is therefore judgement,
  not lint — and there were no false positives to discard.
- **Not run:** the browser-overlay pass (it needs script injection into the page). The captures
  below stand in for it; say so rather than implying an overlay was seen.
- **Grounding:** `PRODUCT.md`, written the same evening, so findings could be judged against what
  the product is *for* (a replacement is only offered where the slot is genuinely free) and not
  only against its drawings.

**Coverage gained.** My Timetable, Student My Timetable, Cohort Timetable, Venue Timetable and
Replacement Arrangement — 390 under touch plus 1440, dark where the surface ships dark.
**Evidence gap, stated rather than papered over:** the Student 390 capture came back **403**, shot
with a lecturer's session; that surface was judged from its drawing and its source only.

**Health score — 30/40 (Good).** Strongest axis is error prevention (4/4: the blocked cells, the
disabled Submit that names its reason, and the same gates re-run server-side). Weakest is aesthetic
and minimalist (2/4), caused by the three findings below, not by taste.
