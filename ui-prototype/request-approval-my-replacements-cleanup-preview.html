The 9 changes

  1. 1 — "Total Requests" renamed. It counts only what the filters leave, while the toolbar still says 13 exist.
  2. 2 — the total card loses its green fill and number. Green then only means approved, so the loudest card is the work, as on My Replacements.
  3. 3 — the toolbar counter stops saying "Showing"; two counters both "showing" 13 and 4 read as a contradiction.
  4. 4 — the toggle is shortened to Hide COMPLETED (it named a status the toggle does not know) and the chip reads "Completed hidden". Its × used to say "Exclude…", so removing it showed more — a double negative in a filter row.
  5. 5 — the sort instruction line is deleted. The active header already draws ▲.
  6. 6 — an empty requested slot says "Not requested" instead of "—".
  7. 7 — the status pill's hover text says it is a status. It is a plain span wearing a button's outline.
  8. 8 — the STAFF column drops the staff ID and the email. Both stay in the request popup, where there is room to read them.
  9. 9 — the COURSE column drops the course name and shows AMSE2003 (T). The name stays in the popup and the search box still finds it — type a name, or the new AMSE2003 (T) form.

The act-on-several bar and its tick column were proposed, then dropped at your request — so the table has no tick boxes and nothing sits above it but the filters.

Deliberately unchanged

  • the four-word status chip repeated down every row
  • the icon-only Action column — three meanings, all on hover
  • zeros under a status filter ("Approved 0" can be false)
  • "Total Reviewed" — it re-adds its two neighbours plus completed
  • "Reset Filters" vs "Clear all" keeping two different jobs
  • the number cards staying below the pager
  • "6 classes" sitting beside "8 hours"

Withdrawn — not defects

  • there is no urgency line in the Original Slot cell; measured on :8000, only Requested Slot carries one
  • the two tables already share column order; the extra columns differ because one page is your own classes and the other is other people's requests
